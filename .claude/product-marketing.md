# Product marketing context: "מחיר אמת" (for the marketing skills)

Read first (they win over any skill): `docs/agents/RULES.md`, `docs/agents/AUDIENCE.md`, `docs/agents/MARKETING.md`.

- **What it is:** an Israeli deals channel + website that only publishes VERIFIED deals: price checked on the store's page,
  a real saving of at least 5% vs the store's earlier price or the next cheapest store, price history, cross-store comparison.
  Promise: "מחיר שנבדק, לא מבצע מזויף".
- **Where:** Telegram channel https://t.me/mechiremet (new, ~0 subscribers on 2026-11-23); website
  https://gregokrol.github.io/Web_Scraping/ (deals, browse by store and category, search); Instagram page planned.
- **Niche:** technology, gaming, computers, home (the website shows all categories of 14 Israeli stores).
- **Audience:** men 22-45, single and in couples, all of Israel. Hebrew.
- **Voice:** matter-of-fact, a little humour, like a friend who knows tech. No shouting, no "!!!".
- **Money:** affiliate links - no program approved yet (AliExpress pending). Disclosure: "ייתכן שחלק מהקישורים הם קישורי שותפים".
- **Team:** the owner alone, ~30-45 minutes a day, no ad budget. Videos: the owner's voice + screen recording, or an
  AI presenter that is LABELLED as AI.
- **Never:** fake scarcity or deadlines, fear of missing out as pressure, invented reviews or numbers, "cheapest in Israel"
  without proof, bought followers, spam, posting anything without the owner's approval, 18+/tobacco/vape/drugs/alcohol/weapons.
- **Tools:** do not install anything (npx, pip, accounts, paid services) without the owner's approval. Answer in Hebrew.
